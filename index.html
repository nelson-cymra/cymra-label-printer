In the print dialog choose the SATO, pick the matching stock, set Scale 100% and Margins: None, and turn off headers and footers. Choosing Save as PDF instead writes a PDF at exactly the stock size.

Both stocks are built for a driver that does no turning of its own: in Printers & scanners → SATO CL4-SXR TT305 → Printing preferences → Page Setup set Rotation 0° - Portrait and pick the stock you are printing. In the browser's own print dialog leave Layout alone — it reads Portrait for the 45 × 218 stock and Landscape for the 25 × 10, because that label is wider than it is tall. Forcing Portrait there turns the 25 × 10 page on its side and clips the label down its right edge.
